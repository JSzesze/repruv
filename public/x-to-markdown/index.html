Accepted links

Repruv accepts public status links from x.com and twitter.com. Protected, deleted, or unavailable posts cannot be converted. 

https://x.com/username/status/123456789

Portable result

The result uses ordinary Markdown instead of an embedded social widget. It is ready for a research note, changelog, knowledge base, or repository.

External source

X conversion relies on a public third-party source and may become unavailable independently. Repruv does not authenticate as you or access private posts.
